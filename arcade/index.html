Arcade cabinet

A playable game on a 3D cabinet: rendered to a texture, shown through a CRT shader.

Drag to look around · Click the screen to play

How it works

Five pieces of Three.js

  • A scene inside a sceneThe game is its own Three.js scene with an orthographic camera, drawn every frame into a 224 × 288 WebGLRenderTarget: the resolution of a 1980s vertical arcade monitor.
  • CRT shaderA GLSL ShaderMaterial samples that texture with barrel curvature, a slight RGB convergence error, phosphor glow, one scanline per game row, an aperture-grille mask and a vignette. Toggle CRT to compare.
  • HDR bloomThe screen, the marquee and the neon trim render brighter than white, so UnrealBloomPass picks up only those before tone mapping. Toggle Bloom to compare.
  • Raycast controlsThe pointer is raycast against the curved screen. The hit's UV coordinate moves the paddle, so dragging on the glass works on a phone too.
  • Blender to glTFThe cabinet loads models/cabinet.glb when it's there. A mesh named Screen gets the CRT material; Joystick and the buttons move with the game.
