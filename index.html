Dandee · Three Lab

Three.js experiments

Small, finished pieces that each show one part of building for the 3D web: shaders, physics, scroll-driven storytelling, product configurators and getting models out of Blender. Built with Three.js, TypeScript and Vite.

  • Play it

    Arcade

    Eight of my games on a row of 3D cabinets, from a Python platformer port to mini golf with real physics. Each game renders to a texture and reaches its tube through a GLSL CRT shader, with HDR bloom on the neon.

    Render targets · GLSL · Bloom · Lazy loading Open
  • Scroll it

    Soundline

    A scroll-driven product page for made-up headphones. They turn, come apart into seven labelled layers, cancel the noise around them and change colour, with a soundtrack that builds as you scroll.

    GSAP ScrollTrigger · Lenis · GLSL · Web Audio OpenCode
  • Restyle it

    Heroline

    Five interactive hero sections, each one a shader: liquid gradient, particle logo, image distortion, morphing blob and text through glass. Restyle any of them live and share it as a link.

    GLSL · GPU particles · Transmission · onBeforeCompile OpenCode
  • Plan it

    Roomline

    A room planner at real sizes. Draw rooms, connect them into a house, add furniture and see what fits: it checks walkways, door swings and wardrobe space as you go, with a catalogue editor for the shop.

    React Three Fiber · Fit rules · glTF upload · Share links OpenCode
  • Play it

    Puttline

    Nine themed holes of mini golf with real ball physics, each with a risky hole-in-one shortcut. Every hole is proved finishable both ways by a solver. Toot, jump and redo once a hole.

    Rapier physics · Bloom · Solver-tested levels OpenCodeIn the arcade
